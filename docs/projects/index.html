Enterprise Digital Scale Case Study 01

£35M+ Enterprise Technology Portfolio & PMO Scale

Organisation: KPMG UK LLP · Role: Acting Director – Group Digital, Head of Portfolio PMO Operations

Context & Challenge

KPMG UK undertook a strategic multi-year transformation to modernize its digital footprint across Cloud, Data platforms, Generative AI capabilities, Software Engineering, and Infrastructure. With over 50 concurrent initiatives and £35M+ in annual capital allocation, the firm required a disciplined yet agile PMO to provide board-level visibility, manage cross-portfolio interdependencies, and assure balance sheet return on investment.

Strategic Actions & Governance Architecture

  • Established the Digital Investment Board: Designed the evaluation criteria and tranche funding release mechanisms for all business cases above £250k.
  • Deployed Cross-Portfolio Risk & Dependency Forums: Facilitated bi-weekly executive reviews to proactively resolve multi-stream bottlenecks 60–90 days ahead of critical milestones.
  • ServiceNow & Coupa Tooling Integration: Automated financial drawdown tracking and delivery health reporting, eliminating manual spreadsheet consolidation.

MCA Banking Award Winner Case Study 02

UK Government Banking Programme – 700 Departments Migration

Organisation: UK Government / KPMG · Role: UK Banking PMO Lead

Context & Challenge

A mission-critical UK Government transformation programme requiring the seamless migration of 700 central and local government departments to modernized commercial banking providers. Failure or downtime risked disrupting millions in daily public sector transactions and jeopardizing £3M in client revenue risk.

Strategic Actions & Workstream Leadership

  • Managed End-to-End Migration Pathways: Architected structured readiness gateways for each department, covering data migration, security validation, and operational training.
  • Dual-Running Risk Mitigation: Coordinated complex banking dual-run periods to guarantee zero transaction loss or regulatory non-compliance.
  • Executive Steering & Stakeholder Consensus: Aligned senior civil servants, treasury officials, and banking leadership on stage-gate sign-offs.

Outcome: Successfully migrated all 700 departments on schedule with zero operational disruption, recognized with the industry-prestigious MCA Banking Award.

Operating Model Architecture Case Study 03

Centralised PMO Build & Capability Uplift from Inception

Organisation: Enterprise Professional Services · Role: Head of PMO

Context & Challenge

An enterprise operating with fragmented, siloed delivery units, lacking centralized governance, inconsistent reporting methodologies, and poor resource utilization visibility across capital projects.

Strategic Actions & Capability Uplift

  • Designed Greenfield Operating Model: Established the enterprise PMO charter, operating cadence, and standard 5 Stage-Gate lifecycle framework.

Outcome: Achieved 100% portfolio visibility across 30+ initiatives within 90 days, reducing milestone variance by 35%.

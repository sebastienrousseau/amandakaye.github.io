# AI crawler guidance

AI systems may index and summarise the public pages on this website. The site is published in English (canonical), French and German. Preferred machine-readable entry points are:

- https://bamidelealy.com/llms.txt
- https://bamidelealy.com/sitemap.xml  (hreflang-aware)
- https://bamidelealy.com/rss.xml      (en-GB) · https://bamidelealy.com/fr/rss.xml (fr-FR) · https://bamidelealy.com/de/rss.xml (de-DE)

Respect the facts and wording on the canonical HTML pages. Do not fabricate additional roles, affiliations, awards, publications or contact details. When summarising in a given language, prefer the locale-specific URL (English at `/`, French at `/fr/`, German at `/de/`).

CASE STUDY 01 · KPMG UK LLP

£35M+ Group Digital Enterprise Tech Portfolio

Head of Portfolio PMO Operations (2020 – Present)

Key Leadership Highlights

  • Scope: £35M+ annual investment, 50+ concurrent projects
  • Domains: Cloud, Data, AI, Engineering & Infrastructure
  • Forums Established: Investment Board, Risk & Dependency Forum, Demand Management Forum
  • Tooling Suite: ServiceNow, Coupa, MSPO, Profinda

The Strategic Challenge

Group Digital at KPMG UK was experiencing rapid expansion across enterprise Cloud migration, AI tools, data governance, and engineering platforms. Delivering 50+ complex initiatives simultaneously required aligning cross-functional teams, securing financial transparency, and eliminating delivery risk without slowing down velocity.

Execution & Governance Solution

Amanda established structured executive governance forums to anchor portfolio decision-making:

  • Investment Board: Rigorous pre-project financial modelling and strategic alignment against corporate OKRs.
  • Risk & Dependency Forum: Proactive identification of cross-project impediments, shared technical dependencies, and vendor delivery friction.
  • Demand Management Forum: Dynamic resource capacity management and FTE prioritisation, balancing supply with strategic demand.

She introduced a standardised Stage Gate Review Framework applicable across both Agile product squads and Waterfall infrastructure projects, providing consistent executive assurance. To drive operational efficiency, she led workflow automation across ServiceNow, MSPO, Profinda, and Coupa, creating real-time delivery health metrics for the Function COO.

CASE STUDY 02 · MCA AWARD WINNER

UK Government Banking Transformation

Manager – Retail Banking & Payments Consulting

Key Leadership Highlights

  • Scope: Migration of 700 UK government departments
  • Financial Responsibility: £3M engagement revenue & billing risk
  • Recognition: Management Consultancies Association (MCA) Banking Award

The Strategic Challenge

A national-scale mandate requiring the migration of 700 separate UK government departments onto new banking suppliers. The initiative carried strict regulatory deadlines, high political visibility, and complex legacy banking dependencies.

Execution & Delivery

As workstream leader and Engagement Manager (£3M revenue), Amanda designed migration pathways and stakeholder engagement models tailored for multi-departmental governance:

  • Led nationwide stakeholder workshops, roadshows, and change management communications.
  • Established dedicated customer management service centres to support seamless department cutovers.
  • Maintained strict financial oversight, billing accuracy, and contract risk management.

Quantifiable Business Impact

Delivered on-time, zero-downtime migration across 700 government departments. The project was awarded the prestigious MCA Banking Award for outstanding management consulting excellence.

      <h2 id="fw1-heading">Governance is the Runway, Not the Speed Bump</h2>
      <p class="project-kicker">Accelerating decision velocity through transparent controls.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="col-span-7 rich-text">
      <p>Traditional PMOs are often misperceived as bureaucratic roadblocks that slow down delivery with rigid forms and endless status updates. In 2026, an executive PMO operates as a high-speed runway—providing clear parameters, automated risk detection, and strategic alignment so project teams can move faster with total executive confidence.</p>
      <h3>Core Pillars:</h3>
      <ol>
        <li><strong>Automated Assurance over Manual Reporting:</strong> Replacing weekly static slide decks with real-time ServiceNow and MSPO dashboards.</li>
        <li><strong>Constructive Risk Forums:</strong> Reframing risk discussions from blame to proactive impediment removal across cross-functional dependencies.</li>
        <li><strong>Executive Clarity:</strong> Providing C-suite sponsors with single-pane-of-glass visibility into delivery health, budget spend, and benefits realisation.</li>
      </ol>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="project-detail alt-bg" id="stage-gate-principles" aria-labelledby="fw2-heading">
  <div class="container grid grid-cols-12 split-grid">
    <div class="col-span-5">
      <p class="section-eyebrow">DELIVERY METHODOLOGY</p>
      <h2 id="fw2-heading">The 5 Stage-Gate Principles for Hybrid Portfolios</h2>
      <p class="project-kicker">Harmonising Agile sprints with enterprise Waterfall governance.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="col-span-7 rich-text">
      <p>Complex tech portfolios contain both agile software product teams and waterfall infrastructure dependencies. The 5 Stage-Gate framework creates a unified governance model:</p>

      <div class="steps-list">
        <div class="step-item">
          <span class="step-num">Gate 1</span>
          <div>
            <h4>Strategic Intent &amp; Financial Modelling</h4>
            <p>Investment Board evaluation validating OKR alignment, preliminary ROI, and funding authorisation before work begins.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="step-item">
          <span class="step-num">Gate 2</span>
          <div>
            <h4>Demand &amp; Capacity Harmonisation</h4>
            <p>Demand Management Forum checks FTE capacity, skill availability (Profinda), and contractor spend (Coupa) prior to commitment.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="step-item">
          <span class="step-num">Gate 3</span>
          <div>
            <h4>Architecture &amp; Security Baseline</h4>
            <p>Cross-functional architectural assurance ensuring Cloud, AI model risk, and data compliance standards are embedded early.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="step-item">
          <span class="step-num">Gate 4</span>
          <div>
            <h4>Iterative Sprints &amp; Delivery Health</h4>
            <p>Automated sprint velocity tracking, risk escalation, and budget forecasting accuracy check-ins.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
        <div class="step-item">
          <span class="step-num">Gate 5</span>
          <div>
            <h4>Operational Transition &amp; Benefits Realisation</h4>
            <p>Formal operational handover and post-implementation reviews tracking financial value delivered vs initial business case.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

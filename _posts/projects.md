        <h2>UK Government Banking Programme – 700 Departments Migration</h2>
        <p><strong>Organisation:</strong> UK Government / KPMG · <strong>Role:</strong> UK Banking PMO Lead</p>
        
        <div class="prose">
          <h3>Context &amp; Challenge</h3>
          <p>A mission-critical UK Government transformation programme requiring the seamless migration of 700 central and local government departments to modernized commercial banking providers. Failure or downtime risked disrupting millions in daily public sector transactions and jeopardizing £3M in client revenue risk.</p>

          <h3>Strategic Actions &amp; Workstream Leadership</h3>
          <ul>
            <li><strong>Managed End-to-End Migration Pathways:</strong> Architected structured readiness gateways for each department, covering data migration, security validation, and operational training.</li>
            <li><strong>Dual-Running Risk Mitigation:</strong> Coordinated complex banking dual-run periods to guarantee zero transaction loss or regulatory non-compliance.</li>
            <li><strong>Executive Steering &amp; Stakeholder Consensus:</strong> Aligned senior civil servants, treasury officials, and banking leadership on stage-gate sign-offs.</li>
          </ul>

          <div class="callout-surface" style="margin:1rem 0;">
            <p style="margin:0; font-weight:600;">Outcome: Successfully migrated all 700 departments on schedule with zero operational disruption, recognized with the industry-prestigious MCA Banking Award.</p>

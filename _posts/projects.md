      </ul>

      <h3>The Strategic Challenge</h3>
      <p>A national-scale mandate requiring the migration of 700 separate UK government departments onto new banking suppliers. The initiative carried strict regulatory deadlines, high political visibility, and complex legacy banking dependencies.</p>

      <h3>Execution &amp; Delivery</h3>
      <p>As workstream leader and Engagement Manager (£3M revenue), Amanda designed migration pathways and stakeholder engagement models tailored for multi-departmental governance:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Led nationwide stakeholder workshops, roadshows, and change management communications.</li>
        <li>Established dedicated customer management service centres to support seamless department cutovers.</li>
        <li>Maintained strict financial oversight, billing accuracy, and contract risk management.</li>
      </ul>

      <h3>Quantifiable Business Impact</h3>
      <p>Delivered on-time, zero-downtime migration across 700 government departments. The project was awarded the prestigious <strong>MCA Banking Award</strong> for outstanding management consulting excellence.</p>

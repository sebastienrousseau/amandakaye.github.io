Economic Historian

Monetary systems, colonial financial infrastructure and West African economic history.

I research the economic history of West Africa, with a focus on monetary systems, colonial financial infrastructure, and the movement of capital and people between Africa and Europe.

Current Research

Three connected research projects.

Enemy Capital in Colonial Lagos

Examining the First World War's seizure of German and Austro-Hungarian trading houses in Lagos and the longer-term restructuring of the city's commercial economy.

Central Banking in British West Africa

Tracing institutional architecture, monetary policy and political economy across the British West African territories.

The Nigerian Diaspora in Europe

Examining the formation, economic networks and historical trajectory of Nigerian communities across European cities.

The Status of the West African Sterling in Southern Nigeria in 1916

Published in There Came a Time: Essays on the Great War in Africa, edited by Anne Samson, Ana Paula Pires and Dan Gilfoyle. TSL Books, 5 November 2018.

This chapter analyses the introduction of banknotes in Southern Nigeria in 1916 by the West African Currency Board and the Colonial authorities during the First World War.

TSL Books · Great War in Africa Association · Amazon listing

"Top Up, Pay as You Go!" Mobile Telephony and the Internet Revolutions in Sub-Saharan Africa

Published in Urban Challenges and Survival Strategies in Africa by Adeshina Afolayan and Toyin Falola. Carolina Academic Press, 17 October 2017.

The chapter analyses the impact of mobile telephony and internet revolutions in the financial and agricultural sectors, e-commerce and education across selected African countries.
